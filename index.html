How it works

  1. Allow microphone access.
  2. Choose the source language using the EN/IT toggle.
  3. Speak naturally and watch the captions appear live.
  4. Open the dashboard to display the bilingual overlay.

Notes

  • This version is locked to English and Italian only.
  • English speech produces Italian translation in the overlay.
  • Italian speech produces English translation in the overlay.
  • You can still download an SRT transcript or copy a Framer embed snippet.
